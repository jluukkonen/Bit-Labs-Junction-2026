## 🔑 Crucial Discoveries & Mentor Clarifications

### 1. It is NOT Traditional "Recruitment"
* Recruitment / job placement is **not** Espoo's core worry or priority for this challenge.
* The real problem is **EVENT ADMISSIONS & CURATION**:
  * Espoo hosts flagship innovation and networking events a few times a year (including upcoming tech events).
  * Scale: In peak years, they received up to **5,000 applicants and accepted 1,500**. Recently, an event had **~500 applicants**.
  * The core headache: **"How many of them are actually a good fit?"**
  * They struggle to filter out unqualified, irrelevant applicants while ensuring high-competence, relevant builders and companies get tickets.

### 2. The Failure of Their Current System
* Espoo currently uses an external AI solution that requires applicants to link their **LinkedIn profiles**.
* The current AI scans the LinkedIn profile and tries to calculate a match score.
* **The mentor's verdict:** *"We would like a wiser solution if possible."*
* **Why it fails:** LinkedIn profiles are full of AI-generated buzzwords, inflated claims, and sterile keyword lists. It doesn't reveal whether someone can actually build, what kind of challenges they solve, or whether they will bring real value to the event.

### 3. Mandatory Requirement: Human-in-the-Loop (Final Verification)
* The mentor explicitly emphasized: **A human must be the final reviewer and decision-maker.**
* AI can assist by sorting, summarizing, scoring, and explaining relevance, but the city organizer must have the final sign-off (vital for public sector ethics, EU AI Act compliance, and fairness).

### 4. Before and During the Event (Two Phases)
* **Before the Event:** Admissions filtering and competence curation.
* **During the Event:** Facilitating attendee connections.
* **NO FORCED SHOEHORNING:** The solution must NOT force people or companies into rigid, pre-assigned meetings. The choice of who to talk to must remain up to the attendees. It should be a "softer", organic connection mechanism based on mutual relevance.

### 5. Technical Context & Expectations
* No predefined API or dataset provided by Espoo.
* Mentor expectations are modest (she mentioned expecting basic PowerPoint slides with text and pictures).
* **Our Advantage:** Presenting an interactive, functioning web prototype with simulated applicant curation and an attendee discovery compass will far exceed their expectations.
