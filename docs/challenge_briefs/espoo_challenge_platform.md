# Junction 2026 — City of Espoo: "Rethinking Recruitment"
*Context gathered Fri 13 Nov 2026, ~22:40 (Helsinki). Sources: Junction Platform (challenge page, dashboard, Participant Guide) + Discord #espoo.*

---

## 1. Official Challenge Brief (Junction Platform)
> "How can recruitment and matchmaking events organized by City of Espoo help companies and talent relevantly find and connect with each other, despite AI-generated job search materials and AI-assisted recruitment processes are becoming common?"

- **Prizes:** 1st €1,500 · 2nd €1,000 · 3rd €500
- **Data/APIs:** No dataset, API, hardware, or challenge-specific judging criteria published.

---

## 2. Expanded Brief (Discord #espoo — Marika from Espoo, Fri 20:16)
> "Finding the right talent is becoming increasingly difficult, especially in fast-changing industries and international recruitment. At the same time, talented professionals often struggle to communicate what they can actually contribute, while companies struggle to articulate what they really need.
>
> The City of Espoo works with companies and professionals by organizing recruitment and matchmaking events which are organized online and in person, sometimes also in hybrid format. Despite strong event organizing capabilities, challenges remain in facilitating the right kinds of matches between recruiting companies and jobseeking professionals participating in the events. We believe there is an opportunity to rethink how talent, skills, needs and opportunities are identified, described, communicated and matched.
>
> Your challenge is to imagine and prototype new ways to help companies and talent find more relevant, efficient and meaningful connections in recruitment and matchmaking events organized by the City of Espoo. The solution can focus on any stage of the journey before, during or after a matchmaking event or address the entire process, registering to, attending and following-up after an event."

- **Other #espoo messages:** Melissa from City of Espoo (16:46) — welcome only. Marika ended with "come say hi 👋" (Booth: Floor 5, Booth #7).

## 3. Key Problem Points
- **Two-sided description problem:** Talent can't express what they can actually contribute (AI-inflated CVs all look identical); companies struggle to articulate genuine problem spaces and day-to-day needs.
- **AI noise loss:** AI-generated CVs/applications and AI-assisted hiring make materials look alike, so real signal is getting lost.
- **Context:** Espoo-run recruitment/matchmaking events — online, in-person, and hybrid.
- **Core gap:** Match quality ("right kinds of matches") and admissions curation.
- **Goal words:** *Relevant, efficient, meaningful connections.*
- **Scope:** Open across before (registration/curation), during (attending/connection), after (follow-up).
- **Task:** "Imagine and prototype."

## 4. Junction-Wide Evaluation Criteria
- **Innovation:** New/different vs. other presented ideas.
- **Impact:** Significant societal impact; share of affected people whose problem is solved.
- **Implementation:** Realistic, viable, feasible from all perspectives.
- **Selection Flow:**
  - Challenge winners are picked directly by the partner (**City of Espoo**).
  - Overall Top 5 are picked by peer review, then pitch 3 min + 3 min Q&A to a 7-judge panel.
  - Strongest projects are *"well functioning, but also well designed with a working business model."*
